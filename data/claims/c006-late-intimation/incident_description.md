# Customer Incident Description

My name is Kavya Reddy. On 12 June 2026, at about 2:15 pm, I was driving my Tata Nexon (TS-09-AE-9122) on Project Road in Secunderabad. Traffic was moving very slowly. The car ahead of me — a Maruti Suzuki WagonR — stopped suddenly, and despite braking I could not stop in time and touched its rear gently. The front of my car hit the WagonR's rear bumper.

There was minor damage to my front bumper and bonnet. Both vehicles were drivable and we exchanged insurance details. I did not feel the damage was significant at the time, and I was busy at work, so I did not report it immediately. About a week later, on 18 June 2026, I submitted the claim to VeRoof together with a repair estimate from the service centre.

The estimated repair cost is ₹48,500.
