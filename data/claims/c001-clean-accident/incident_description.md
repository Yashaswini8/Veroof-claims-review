# Customer Incident Description

My name is Rohan Mehta. On the morning of 10 February 2026 at around 9:40 am, I was riding my Honda Activa (MH-12-AB-4482) towards Satara on the Pune–Satara Road. It had rained the night before and the road was wet. A truck ahead of me braked suddenly, and when I braked, my front wheel lost grip and the bike skidded. I fell and the bike hit a roadside tree on its front side.

The impact damaged the front mudguard and scratched the headlamp area. I was not hurt badly. No other vehicle touched my bike, and no other person was involved. I did not ride the bike after the accident. I called my repairer the same day and the bike was towed to Accurate Auto Works, Pune, for inspection and an estimate.

I am claiming for the repair cost of ₹8,400 as per the estimate.
