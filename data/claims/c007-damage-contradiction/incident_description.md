# Customer Incident Description

My name is Manoj Gupta. On 22 July 2026 at about 1:10 pm I was waiting at a red signal on Faizabad Road in Lucknow. My foot was on the brake and my car was stationary. A two-wheeler coming from behind hit my car — the rider struck the **rear of my car** near the rear bumper and then rode off and did not stop.

Afterwards I examined my car. The damage was at the **back**, to the rear bumper and the tail area. There was a scrape and a dent on the rear bumper. The bike rider did not wait and I did not get his number. Because it was a minor matter and neither I nor the rider was hurt, I did not file a police FIR.

I am claiming the repair cost of ₹26,800.
