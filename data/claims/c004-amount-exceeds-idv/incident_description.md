# Customer Incident Description

My name is Priya Sharma. On 5 April 2026 around 5:45 pm I was driving my Hyundai Creta (DL-3C-AQ-7710) on NH-44 near the Karnal bypass. Traffic was slow and I had stopped behind a line of cars. Suddenly a truck behind me — registration FALS/5167/HR — did not stop and hit my car from behind at speed. My car was pushed into the median and hit the guardrail on the left side.

The rear of my car was badly crushed, the left side was damaged, and the side airbags on the left deployed. I was shaken but not seriously hurt. An FIR was registered with the NH-44/Haryana police. My car was towed to Sharma Motors, Karnal, where the surveyor inspected it and gave an estimate of ₹7,80,000 for repairs. With towing and other charges, my total claim is ₹8,10,000.

Both the repairer and the surveyor say the damage is so heavy that it may be cheaper for the insurer to declare the car a total loss.
