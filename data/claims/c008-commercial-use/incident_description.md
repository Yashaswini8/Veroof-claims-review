# Customer Incident Description

My name is Amit Verma. On 3 September 2026, at about 10:40 pm, I was driving my Maruti Ertiga (MH-04-BV-7730) on the Western Express Highway service road in Andheri East, Mumbai. I was ending my shift and heading home after dropping off my last passenger for the night.

At that hour the road was mostly empty. The Ertiga is used by my family but I also use it to run a small airport-transfer service to supplement income — on this particular trip I had just dropped a fare at the domestic airport and was returning. On the service road, I misjudged a curve and the front of the car hit the road divider.

The front bumper, underbody and some lights were damaged. The car was not drivable so I had it towed to Andre Auto Services in Andheri East, where the estimate came to ₹58,000. I am claiming this amount.
