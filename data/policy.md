# VeRoof Motor Insurance Policy

**Policy Document — VeRoof Insurance Ltd.**
**Cover Sheet**

This document constitutes the full terms and conditions of the VeRoof Motor Insurance Policy. The policy is a standard package motor insurance contract covering **Private Cars** and **Two-Wheelers** against **Own-Damage** (accidental damage, fire, theft) as set out below. In this policy:

- "We", "us", "our" means VeRoof Insurance Ltd.
- "You", "your", "yourself" means the policyholder named in the Certificate of Insurance.
- "Insured Vehicle" means the vehicle described in the Certificate of Insurance and the schedule.
- "IDV" means Insured Declared Value, being the maximum amount we will pay for a total loss (including theft) of the Insured Vehicle.
- "FIR" means First Information Report registered with the police.
- "Policy Period" means the period of cover stated in the schedule, from the Policy Start Date to the Policy End Date.

Clauses in this policy are numbered by Section and Clause. The number format is **Clause X.Y**. Cross-references use this number format throughout.

---

## Section 1 — Cover

### Clause 1.1 — Cover for Private Cars and Two-Wheelers

Subject to the terms, conditions and exclusions of this policy, we will indemnify you against loss or damage to the Insured Vehicle caused by:

(a) an **accident** (collision, impact, overturning, or running off the road) occurring during the Policy Period;

(b) **fire, explosion, self-ignition or lightning**;

(c) **theft or attempted theft** of the Insured Vehicle reported to the police in accordance with Clause 6.2;

(d) **riots, strikes, malicious damage and terrorism** (subject to the terrorism exclusion in Clause 4.8);

(e) natural calamities — flood, earthquake, landslide, cyclone, hailstorm, rockslide.

We will indemnify you for the cost of repair of the Insured Vehicle up to the limits in Clause 3, or, in the case of total loss or theft, the IDV as defined in Clause 3.1, less the applicable excess and depreciation per Clause 3.3.

### Clause 1.2 — No Third-Party Liability Cover

This policy covers only own-damage to the Insured Vehicle. It does **not** cover third-party liability, injury to third parties, or damage to any other person's property. Where the accident involved another vehicle or party, you must file a **First Information Report (FIR)** with the police and submit a copy of the same with your claim.

## Section 2 — Period of Cover and Its Territory

### Clause 2.1 — Policy Period

The policy covers the Insured Vehicle only for loss or damage occurring **within the Policy Period**, that is, between the Policy Start Date and the Policy End Date stated in the Certificate of Insurance. Any incident occurring **before the Policy Start Date or after the Policy End Date** is not covered.

### Clause 2.2 — Territory

The cover extends only to incidents occurring within the territorial limits of the country of issue. Claims arising from incidents outside this territory are not covered.

---

## Section 3 — Sum Insured and Amounts

### Clause 3.1 — Insured Declared Value (IDV)

The IDV of the Insured Vehicle is declared by you at inception of the policy and is stated in the Certificate of Insurance. For the first year of registration the IDV is the manufacturer's listed showroom price less depreciation. For subsequent years the IDV is computed as the previous year's IDV less depreciation on the basis prescribed by the regulator. The IDV represents the **maximum amount payable** in the event of total loss of or theft of the Insured Vehicle.

### Clause 3.2 — Claims Not to Exceed IDV

We will not pay more than the **IDV** for a total loss or theft of the Insured Vehicle. Where the claimed or estimated repair amount is close to or exceeds the IDV of the vehicle, the vehicle is assessed for total loss rather than repair, and any recommendation to pay an amount **exceeding the IDV must be flagged** and requires approval at a level above the claims officer. No claim may be recommended for approval at an amount exceeding the IDV without this flag.

### Clause 3.3 — Depreciation and Excess

(a) **Depreciation**: The value of replaced parts is depreciated per the tariff fixed at the time of loss, based on the age of the vehicle.
(b) **Excess**: For every claim, the compulsory and voluntary excess stated on the Certificate of Insurance is deducted from the loss amount payable. The excess is not refundable.
(c) **Deductible**: Where a claim is intimated after the expiry of the intimation period in Clause 6.1, an additional deductible applies as provided in Clause 6.1.

## Section 4 — Exclusions

### Clause 4.1 — Driving Without a Valid License

We will not be liable for any loss, damage or liability caused or sustained while the Insured Vehicle is being **driven by a person not holding a valid and effective driving license** that covers the class of vehicle insured. A learner's license is valid only when the driver holds the license and is accompanied by a person holding a valid license for that class of vehicle as required by law.

### Clause 4.2 — Driving Under the Influence

We will not be liable for any loss, damage or liability while the Insured Vehicle is being **driven under the influence of intoxicating liquor or drugs** or while the driver's mental or physical faculties are impaired by any substance.

### Clause 4.3 — Commercial Use of a Private Vehicle

We will not be liable for any loss, damage or liability arising while the Insured Vehicle is being **used for hire or reward** or for any **commercial purpose** when the Insured Vehicle is insured under a private-use-only policy. Carrying passengers for hire or payment, delivery of goods against payment, or use in the business of transporting persons or goods for a fee are all treated as commercial use.

### Clause 4.4 — Pre-Existing Damage

We will not be liable for loss or damage that **existed prior to the Policy Period** or that is a known defect or previously repaired area not reported at inception. Repairs unrelated to the incident reported are not covered.

### Clause 4.5 — Driving the Vehicle Beyond a Reasonable Distance after the Incident

We will not be liable for loss or damage occurring **after the Insured Vehicle has been driven more than a reasonable distance following the incident** without the vehicle being examined. You must not continue to use the vehicle after an incident in a manner that aggravates the damage.

### Clause 4.6 — Unauthorized Use by a Nominated Driver

We will not be liable when the Insured Vehicle is used by a person other than the named insured, a family member, or a person driving with the permission of the insured, except with our prior consent.

### Clause 4.7 — War and Nuclear Risks

We will not be liable for loss or damage occasioned by war, invasion, act of foreign enemy, hostilities (whether war be declared or not), civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection, military or usurped power, or confiscation or nationalisation or requisition by order of any government or public authority. Nuclear loss or damage is excluded in all circumstances.

### Clause 4.8 — Civil Commotion, Riot and Terrorism Sub-Limit

Subject to Clause 1.1(d), loss or damage by riots, strikes, malicious damage and terrorism is covered only up to a **sub-limit of 50% of IDV exclusive of GST**, and only when the incident is notified to the police and an FIR is registered within the time limits in Clause 6.2.

### Clause 4.9 — Wilful Neglect or Intentional Damage

We will not be liable for loss or damage caused by **wilful neglect or intentional damage** by you or any person acting with your consent, or where the claim is fraudulent or supported by false or forged documents.

## Section 5 — Conditions and Duties

### Clause 5.1 — Your Duty of Disclosure

You must disclose every material fact relevant to the cover. Failure to disclose a material fact, or making a misrepresentation, may render the policy void from inception and we may refuse all claims.

### Clause 5.2 — Care of the Vehicle

You must maintain the Insured Vehicle in a good state of repair and take all reasonable steps to safeguard the vehicle and prevent loss or damage.

### Clause 5.3 — Salvage and Subrogation

Where we pay a claim, we are entitled to salvage of the damaged vehicle and to all rights and remedies of the insured against any person in respect of the loss. You must not abandon the insured vehicle to us except by written notice.

### Clause 5.4 — No Admission of Liability

You must not admit liability, negotiate, or settle any claim arising out of an incident without our written consent. Any voluntary damage you cause to avoid liability is not covered.

### Clause 5.5 — Registration and Licence Documents

You must, at the time of loss, be in possession of valid registration papers (RC) of the Insured Vehicle and produce them on request. Failure to produce the registration and licence documents on request is a condition precedent to liability for the claim.

### Clause 5.6 — Duty to Minimize Loss

You must take all reasonable steps to minimize the loss and to secure the vehicle after an incident. Costs reasonably incurred in minimizing the loss are payable.

## Section 6 — Claim Intimation and Procedure

### Clause 6.1 — Claim Intimation Period

(a) In the event of an **accident** to the Insured Vehicle, you must give notice to us **within 48 hours** of the incident or as soon as reasonably possible thereafter.
(b) In the event of **theft** of the Insured Vehicle, an **FIR must be registered with the police within 24 hours** of the discovery of the theft, and the claim must be intimated to us **within 7 days** of the theft or discovery thereof.
(c) Where notice is given after the above periods, we may still consider the claim at our discretion, but an **additional deductible of 10% of the assessed loss, subject to a minimum of the compulsory excess**, applies and the delay must be explained to our satisfaction in writing.
(d) The date of intimation recorded in our systems is the date of receipt of this notice; the date of filing the claim form with us is the date used to test the intimation period.

### Clause 6.2 — Theft: FIR and Police Procedure

In the event of theft of the Insured Vehicle, you must:
(a) register an **FIR within 24 hours** of the discovery of the theft with the police station having jurisdiction over the place of occurrence;
(b) intimate the claim to us **within 7 days**; and
(c) provide the police release certificate/documents on recovery of the vehicle.

### Clause 6.3 — Required Documents for Accident Claims

For an accident claim, the following documents are required to be submitted:
(a) duly filled and signed **Claim Form**;
(b) the **Repair Estimate** from a quantum surveyor or the repairer;
(c) a copy of the **FIR** where a third party or another vehicle is involved;
(d) copy of **RC (Registration Certificate)** or copy of policy schedule.

### Clause 6.4 — Required Documents for Theft Claims

For a theft claim, the following documents are required to be submitted:
(a) duly filled and signed **Claim Form**;
(b) the **FIR** lodged with the police within the time limits of Clause 6.2;
(c) attested copy of the **RC (Registration Certificate)**;
(d) copy of the **Policy Schedule** or Certificate of Insurance;
(e) **Key Handover** — original keys of the Insured Vehicle along with the key number / master key;
(f) **NOC (Non-Objection Certificate) from the financier**, where the vehicle is hypothecated to a financier;
(g) **Iron documents / blank cheque** or such other documents as the recovery of the vehicle may require.

### Clause 6.5 — Survey and Verification

We may appoint a surveyor to assess the damage or loss. The surveyor's report, repair estimate and bills form the basis of our settlement. You must make the Insured Vehicle available for inspection at a place and time we reasonably require. Non-availability of the vehicle for inspection may delay or invalidate the claim.

### Clause 6.6 — Fraud and False Documents

Where the claim is fraudulent, or is supported by false, forged or manipulated documents, or the incident is staged, the claim shall be repudiated in full and we shall report the matter to the appropriate authorities.

## Section 7 — Settlement

### Clause 7.1 — Basis of Settlement

We will settle a claim by payment of the cost of repair (less depreciation, excess and applicable deductibles) up to the limits of this policy, or by payment of the IDV less excess for a total loss or theft, whichever is applicable. Settlement is made to you or, where the vehicle is hypothecated, jointly with the financier.

### Clause 7.2 — Claim Review Responsibility

All claims are subject to review against the documents submitted. Where the submitted documents are incomplete, inconsistent, or where a claim falls in an uncertain grey area, the claim **must be escalated to a human investigator** rather than decided by an automatic process. Claims should not be recommended for approval where required documents are missing without the missing items being requested first.
